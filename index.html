Which crops should you grow next?

Field Shift compares two-year crop plans for your field. It uses NASA climate data for your exact location, your soil and water, and what matters most to you. It then shows how much water, soil health and income each plan could mean.

How it works

1. NASA climateTemperature, rain, sunlight and soil moisture for your point come live from NASA POWER.
2. Crop waterSunlight and heat give the daily water loss. Each crop's needs (FAO-56) give its monthly water use. Rain is taken away to find the irrigation needed.
3. Heat riskWe check each crop's sensitive weeks, such as wheat grain filling in March or cotton flowering in July, against your temperatures.
4. Your choicesSoil, water and your five priorities decide the final ranking.

Field Shift helps you plan. It does not replace advice. Check varieties and sowing dates with your local agriculture extension office.
